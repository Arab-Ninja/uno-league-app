Subject: Automatic statistics for every match played at your centre: UNO Vision is looking for pilot centres

Hello,

Your pitches are already filmed. We propose to get more out of that footage.

UNO Vision is an AI video-analysis tool we are developing for UNO League, our amateur futsal league. From the recording of a match, it rebuilds the pitch seen from above, tracks every player and the ball, and deduces goals, shots, passes and saves, along with what no referee can note: top speed, distance covered, accelerations, possession.

It is designed for the corner cameras already installed in five-a-side centres, at their current quality: there is nothing to install. There is no facial recognition: players are identified by their bib and by the organiser, and every proposed action is validated by a person before it is published.

The current version works end to end, from the video to the proposed actions, and we refine it match after match. We are looking for a few pilot centres to fine-tune it in different halls. For you, that means:
- a new service for your customers: the statistics of their match, as in professional football;
- priority access to the tool, and partner terms once it is marketed;
- your centre associated with an innovation, in our communication.

On your side, it would mean sharing a few match videos, under a written agreement that complies with the GDPR: used only to fine-tune the tool, no one identified, nothing published.

Our presentation deck is attached: UNO Vision is on page 9. I would be glad to give you a demonstration, on site or online.
